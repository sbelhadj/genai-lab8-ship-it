# Lab 8 — Ship It

## Student Instructions

**Module:** 8 — Lightweight Deployment & Final Evaluation | **Duration:** 90 min

---

## Context

You've built DevAssist across Labs 1-7: tokenization, attention, prompt contracts, techniques, test-driven prompting, RAG, and security. Now ship it — with evaluation, documentation, and a live demo.

| Phase | Time | Activity |
|-------|------|----------|
